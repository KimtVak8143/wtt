Rules

  • Make exercises harder each week: more reps, slower lowering, shorter rest, harder variation.
  • Runs B and C stay easy. Light evenings stay light.
  • Fat loss is mostly food: 300-500 kcal deficit, about 110 g protein. Track your waist weekly.
